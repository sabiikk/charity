Terms & Conditions

  1. 1

    One full calendar year must pass since the last assistance was received.

  2. 2

    Registration will be considered cancelled if the case is registered on a day not designated for it, for example a resident registering on a day designated for a national, and vice versa.

  3. 3

    Registration will be considered cancelled if cases from the Ajman branch are registered in Ras Al Khaimah and vice versa.

  4. 4

    The required documents must be provided after the researcher contacts the case via phone text messages within a period not exceeding 5 working days.

  5. 5

    Bring the case’s original documents and copies of them for verification.

  6. 6

    Medical cases that require assistance for treatment by the Association will be registered, and a recent medical report must be presented.
